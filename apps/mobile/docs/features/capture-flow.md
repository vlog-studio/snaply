# Capture flow

## User goal and screen flow

Users open capture from the center button of the tab bar, land directly in the viewfinder, choose a 3- or 5-second length inline, and shoot. Capture is **continuous**: each snap is saved and the recorder stays on the viewfinder, ready for the next hold, so the user is never bounced away mid-session. Feedback stays in-camera — the just-captured frame flies up into the snap counter, which then bumps and pops. Leaving is explicit: the ✕ dismisses to the Studio.

```text
(tab bar center button, from any tab)
  -- open capture --> /capture   (root-stack full-screen modal, viewfinder with an inline 3초/5초 toggle)
  -- press-and-hold --> the frame flies into the "스냅 N개" counter, which bumps + pops; stay in the viewfinder --> hold again ...
  -- ✕ (explicit leave) --> / (Studio)
```

**A snap is filed into nothing.** It sits in the library until it is picked into a movie — by hand in the [Snap library](snaps.md) or by a [template](movie-templates.md); there is no daily roll and no automatic collection ([product concept](../../../../docs/decisions/product-concept.md) §1).

The supported capture options are owned by `entities/capture-session`:

- Durations: 3 or 5 seconds; an invalid or missing value normalizes to 3, which seeds the recorder's initial state
- No look (mood) option: the look belongs to the finished movie, chosen on the movie screen with the whole thing in view, rather than to each fragment as it is shot ([product concept](../../../../docs/decisions/product-concept.md) §5)

## Capture options

The duration is tuned inline in the viewfinder, not on a separate setup screen. `/capture` opens straight into the recorder (`pages/capture-record`), which owns the selected duration as local React state.

While the recorder is `idle`, a `3초 / 5초` toggle sits above the shutter: two tape chips, the chosen one butter tape and the other a dark smoky strip in cream ink (a radio pair for screen readers). Selecting one triggers selection haptics on iOS and resets the on-screen countdown. The toggle is hidden once a hold starts (`recording`/`saving`) and in the `review` stage. Because the option can only change while idle, each snap is recorded under the duration shown at the moment the hold began.

**The option is a maximum, not the snap's length.** Recording runs only while the shutter is held, so a released finger ends it early and the file is shorter than 3 or 5 seconds. `Snap.durationSec` is therefore read back from the saved file rather than set to the option (see [Snap library](snaps.md#data-model)); the option is only the fallback for a file the platform cannot measure.

## Camera recording and review

| Capability | Status | Platform and behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark-pinned viewfinder chrome | `Functional` | The whole screen is wrapped in `<ThemeScope scheme="dark">` and mounts its own light status bar and light Android navigation-bar buttons (`expo-navigation-bar` `<NavigationBar style="light" />`; the app-wide bar follows the scheme, so under the light theme its 3-button glyphs were dark over the ground under the shutter): its ground is the camera feed and near-black scrims, so its chrome ignores the app theme mode and always uses the dark palette. Not yet checked on a three-button-navigation phone. |
| Camera and microphone permission flow | `Functional` | iOS and Android request missing permissions and can open system settings after denial. The denied screen's button reads 카메라·마이크 권한 허용 while the OS can still ask, and 설정에서 권한 켜기 once it cannot — the label every permission row in the app uses for opening the OS settings — which opens them. |
| Press-and-hold 3- or 5-second video recording | `Functional` | iOS and Android record at 720p while the shutter is held. Releasing the finger stops the recording early; the native `maxDuration` ends it automatically when the ring completes. Holds of 250ms or less are treated as accidental taps: the temp recording is discarded (never saved) and the screen silently returns to idle. |
| Sound toggle | `Functional` | Recording can be muted; enabling sound requires microphone permission. During review the toggle mutes/unmutes the looping playback without restarting it. |
| Front/back camera toggle | `Functional` | Available while the recorder is idle. |
| Framing guide (SNAP-23) | `Functional` | Off by default; the grid sticker in the top bar (read aloud as 촬영 가이드 켜기 / 촬영 가이드 끄기, drawn selected while on) turns it on for this visit to the screen — leaving the screen turns it off again. While on, rule-of-thirds lines are drawn over the feed and the **previous cut's closing frame** is laid over it at 35% opacity, cover-fitted like the preview, so the next snap can be framed in the same place and size. The previous cut is the most recently captured snap whose file is on this device (`model/previous-cut.ts`, an ordered rule list whose only rule today is "latest" — capture-coach §4.1); each new capture becomes the next one's previous cut. Its frame is sampled 120ms before the measured end through `shared/lib/video-thumbnails`. In place of the focus frame, an idle viewfinder shows 앞 컷에 겹쳐 같은 자리에서 찍어 보세요 on a smoke strip, or 격자 선에 맞춰 찍어 보세요 when there is no previous cut yet. The guide never takes a touch and stays up while recording. Seen on the Android emulator (2026-10-30); not yet on a phone. A snap whose recording began while the guide showed a previous cut remembers it as `Snap.linedUpWith` (read when recording starts, since the previous cut moves on to the new snap once it is saved) and sends it as `linedUpWithClientId` when it is registered; the AI recipe edit then prefers cutting the two next to each other (SNAP-24, capture-coach §4.2). Nothing in the app shows the pair. |
| AI reading of the previous cut (SNAP-25) | `Functional` | Only for an account that agreed to snap analysis (`features/analysis-consent`) and never with the mock API. While the guide shows a previous cut, `model/use-previous-cut-read.ts` sends that cut's closing frame — the ghost's own thumbnail, read as base64, never the video — once per cut to `POST /capture-coach/previous-cut` (`api/read-previous-cut.ts`; the server asks `gpt-6-luna` and stores nothing). The answer's line replaces the guide's own on the smoke strip, and its subject spot is drawn as a dashed cream square over the ghost, placed with the same cover fit as the preview (`coverSubjectRect` in `ui/capture-guide.tsx`). Any failure — no consent, the server's switch off, no model key, a slow or malformed answer — leaves the guide's own line, and is not retried for that cut. Seen on the Android emulator against a local server with a model key (2026-10-30): the line and the box appeared, but the answer took about 8 seconds — the server has since turned the model's reasoning off, after which the emulator's request took about 2.6 seconds end to end on the server; not yet on a phone. |
| Saving a snap | `Functional` | On capture completion the temporary recording is moved into the app document directory, its real length and display size are read back from it — after the move rather than beside it, because reading a file being relocated is not a race worth taking — and a snap is created from all of it. How the length and size are measured, and what a snap keeps when its file cannot be read, are in [Snap library](snaps.md#data-model). Owned by `features/capture-moment`. The snap joins no movie. |
| Automatic album copy | `Functional` | With 자동 앨범 저장 on (나 → 앨범 저장, off by default — SNAP-18), `use-capture-session.ts` hands each saved snap to `features/save-snap-to-album`'s `saveCapturedSnapToAlbum`, which adds a copy to the device's album ([Snap library](snaps.md#saving-to-the-album)). The next hold never waits on it, and it never turns a saved capture into a failed one: a copy that could not be made shows its line in the camera screen's error banner (앨범에 저장하려면 사진 권한이 필요해요. · 앨범에 저장하지 못했어요.), below any capture error. It never raises a permission prompt — the switch asked when it was turned on. Walked on the Android emulator (2026-09-29) and on a phone (Galaxy S22 Ultra, 2026-10-09): each capture landed in `Pictures/Snaply` with its capture time as the gallery date; not yet on iOS. |
| Recording where a snap was shot | `Functional` | iOS and Android. While the file is being moved, `features/capture-moment` asks for foreground location permission and reads the current coordinates onto the snap (`Snap.place`). The two run concurrently so a coordinate adds no latency to the save, and **every failure is silent**: a refusal, no fix, a read slower than two seconds, or a throwing adapter all file the snap with no place. The prompt is the OS's own and appears at most once. Nothing in capture reads the value back — it exists for template matching (see [Movie templates](movie-templates.md)). |
| Continuous capture | `Functional` | On success the recorder returns to `idle` and stays on the viewfinder — it never auto-navigates. Feedback is in-camera: a paused frame of the just-captured snap (`ui/capture-flight.tsx`, a print with paper's hard shadow) flies up from the viewfinder into the top "스냅 N개" counter (~480ms, rendered beneath the top bar so it tucks in behind the counter's strip of butter tape); on arrival the displayed count bumps, the counter pops, and a "담김 · 스냅 N개" badge shows for ~1.1s (plus a success haptic). The count on the tape trails the store until the frame lands, so the number rises as it arrives. Reduced motion skips the flight and pop (the count updates and the badge/haptic remain). |
| Leave → Studio | `Functional` | Leaving is explicit: the ✕ dismisses to the **Studio** (not the tab that opened capture), which is where the next step lives. |
| Shooting for an empty template slot | `Functional` | `지금 찍기` on a slot opens this same screen with nothing changed about it — the snap is filed in the library like any other. The template screen is what remembers which slot asked and picks the new snap up on the way back (see [Movie templates](movie-templates.md)). Capture stays ignorant of it on purpose: a capture that behaved differently depending on where it was opened from is a capture that can lose a snap. |
| Select a previous recording | `Functional` | The in-screen recording library (찍은 스냅 · 앱에 저장된 스냅 N개; 아직 찍은 스냅이 없어요 when empty), opened from the `스냅 N` control beside the shutter (read aloud as 찍은 스냅 N개 보기) or, without camera permission, the 찍어둔 스냅 보기 (N) button, can select (enters a preview/review stage, left with 다시 찍기) or delete a locally stored original with the row's 삭제 word (ink on a kraft highlighter — `TextButton` `tone="delete"`; 삭제하는 중… while the file goes). Deleting goes through `features/delete-snap`, so an original already used by a movie is removed from it too (see [Snap library](snaps.md#deleting-an-original)), and asks first on a plain dark sheet inside the library (it was a system alert before 2026-10-19): 스냅을 삭제할까요? / 삭제한 스냅은 되돌릴 수 없어요. over two compact tickets side by side (취소 and a kraft 삭제 stamped VOID; the compact faces keep the VOID close to the end, so 삭제 has room for one line at 360dp — not yet seen on a device at large text) for a recording that never reached the server, or, for one the server still keeps, the snap tab's question — 이 기기에서만 삭제 (the snap stays in the snap tab) or 모든 기기에서 삭제 (it can still come back from 최근 삭제). That alert's wording and what each answer does are owned by [Snap library](snaps.md#from-this-device-only). Which question the sheet asks is judged once, when it opens, so an upload finishing while it is up does not swap the answers under the user's finger. While the sheet is up, the list behind it (head and rows) is out of the screen-reader order — Android `importantForAccessibility="no-hide-descendants"`, iOS `accessibilityElementsHidden` — as it was under the system alert, so TalkBack cannot reach another row's 삭제 or select a row under the sheet; closing the library drops an open question. The rows are ruled like the board, a line under every row and over the first. Either way the recording leaves this list. |
| Web recording | `Not implemented` | The shutter is disabled and the UI explains that recording requires iOS or Android. |

**The smoke over the feed is dark, not the canvas's cream.** The REC strip and the unpicked duration chip sit straight on the live feed, so their ground is a literal dark wash — `media` near-black at 60% (`TapeSmoke`, `rgba(14, 11, 8, 0.6)` in `shared/ui/tape-chip`, the same wash as the extract stage's position readout) — under the dark palette's cream `text`. The canvas draws both as a 14% cream wash (`.cp-tape--smoke`, `.cp-dur-off`); over a white wall the cream countdown on it fell to about 1.1:1, and the unpicked chip, whose ink was `stickerInk`, to about 1:1 over the black viewfinder before the camera is ready. The dark wash gives about 5:1 over a white wall (with the feed's 12% shade under it) — a little darker than the black scrims the countdown (48%) and the durations (36%) sat on before the redesign. Not yet seen on a device over a bright scene.

The page uses four explicit stages: `idle`, `recording`, `saving`, and `review`, presented over a plain near-black surface (decision 6 of the junk-journal design: no paper or tape decoration on the capture screens, only the journal's controls and labels) — round sheet stickers for close, sound, the library and the camera flip; status written on tape in mono (a butter "스냅 N개" counter, a dark smoke REC strip with a cherry dot and the countdown, a kraft "스냅을 저장하는 중…" while saving, a mint "담김 · 스냅 N개" after each capture); the 3초/5초 choice as two tape chips (the unchosen one smoke); errors as the alert card (the design system's "white card" — drawn in the sheet colour, a dark brown under the pinned dark palette, with the message in cherry ink); and a cherry shutter disc with a stitched ring inside an ink ring, turning into a square while held. Recording runs only while the shutter is held, and a progress ring around it (`ui/hold-ring.tsx`, react-native-svg stroke-dashoffset driven by Reanimated) fills linearly over the selected duration, rewinding over 250ms on release. When the OS "reduce motion" setting is on, the ring shows a static partial arc instead of a continuous fill. The accidental-tap threshold is owned by `model/hold-gesture.ts`. The `review` stage is reached only by selecting a recording from the library, not by capturing. Screen readers are not handed the REC countdown as it ticks: none of the stage tapes is a live region, so the voice that reads every second never ends up in the take; the countdown can still be read by moving focus to it. Capture, save, album-copy, and recording-library failures are displayed inside the camera screen and can be dismissed (스냅을 저장하지 못했어요. 다시 시도해 주세요. · 앨범에 저장하지 못했어요. · 찍은 스냅을 불러오지 못했어요.).

Closing the recorder mid-recording stops the camera and discards the in-flight snap; nothing is persisted. The on-screen countdown (`3초`, `2초`, …) is display-only — the actual stop is driven by the native `maxDuration`, so after the counter reaches zero the badge shows a finishing state until the recording completes.

The camera-permission-denied screen (스냅을 찍으려면 카메라 권한이 필요해요.) still exposes the recording library, so users can browse, select (which enters the review stage), and delete saved originals without camera permission. A library failure there (찍은 스냅을 불러오지 못했어요.) shows as the same alert card under its buttons.

## Ownership and dependencies

- `src/features/capture-moment/lib/read-capture-place.ts` owns the location read and the rule that it can never fail a capture. It is the feature's decision to ask, per the boundary in `shared/lib/location`, which stays transport-only.
- `src/pages/capture-record` owns camera lifecycle, camera/microphone permissions, capture-stage orchestration, the inline duration state (idle-only), the in-camera feedback (counter pulse + "담김" badge), and its internal recording-library modal. Those are five separate concerns in `model`, each with its own reason to change:
  - `use-recording-permissions.ts` — camera + microphone permission state, the one automatic request for what is missing, the manual retry, and the settings deep link. It exposes booleans and Korean copy, never `expo-camera`'s permission objects.
  - `use-camera-device.ts` — the camera handle, readiness, facing, and sound. The **only** module in the slice that calls `expo-camera`'s imperative API; the handle never leaves the file (the page attaches the view through `attachCamera`). It publishes the narrow `RecordingDevice` contract the capture run is written against.
  - `use-capture-session.ts` — one capture run: the hold gesture, the `idle`/`recording`/`saving` machine, the display countdown, the selected duration, handing the file to `features/capture-moment`, and then the saved snap to the automatic album copy (`features/save-snap-to-album`). It holds the capture rules and no SDK details.
  - `use-capture-guide.ts` — whether the framing guide is on, the previous cut (`previous-cut.ts`), its closing frame, and the AI's reading of it (`use-previous-cut-read.ts` over `api/read-previous-cut.ts`); `ui/capture-guide.tsx` draws the grid, the ghost, and the subject square.
  - `use-recording-library.ts` — the saved-originals list, the modal, which recording is previewed, and deletion through `features/delete-snap` — everywhere, or from this device only for a recording the server keeps.
  - `use-capture-recorder.ts` composes those five and owns only what spans them: the screen stage (`review` is "a recording is selected"), the single error banner, and leaving/retaking/opening the library/previewing/deleting. The page consumes it and renders.
- `src/shared/lib/haptics` wraps `expo-haptics` with the project's iOS-only guard (`impactFeedback`, `selectionFeedback`, `successFeedback`). Capture and the tab bar's capture button both go through it; neither calls the SDK directly.
- `src/features/capture-moment` owns saving a snap: persisting the file and building snap metadata. It owns its own pending/error state and does not navigate.
- `src/pages/capture-record/ui/capture-flight.tsx` owns the "frame flies into the counter" animation; the recorder page owns the trailing count, counter pop, and badge.
- `src/entities/snap` owns snap metadata and its persisted store.
- `src/entities/capture-session` owns the capture duration type and its route-value normalization.
- `src/features/manage-recordings` owns reusable local-recording state and actions. The library's date lines are formatted by `shared/lib/datetime`, not by this slice.
- `src/shared/ui/video-preview` owns the business-agnostic looping video player used in the review step.
- `src/shared/lib/recording-files` adapts Expo FileSystem and supplies the web fallback; `src/shared/lib/local-store` persists snap metadata as document-directory JSON.

## Persistence and privacy

Original recordings are app-private files in the app's document directory, removed with the app ([Snap library](snaps.md#file-model-and-storage-boundary)). The device's album gets a copy of its own only when the user saves one or turns on the automatic copy ([Snap library](snaps.md#saving-to-the-album)); the app never reads the album. Every saved snap is also uploaded to the backend in the background, because a generation run is made from the server's copies ([Snap library](snaps.md#backend-upload-sync)).

A snap's coordinates stay on the device (SNAP-11): they are stored in the local snap metadata, never uploaded — the upload carries the file with its length, capture time, and id ([Snap library](snaps.md#backend-upload-sync)) — and never turned into a place name. The iOS purpose string in `app.json` states both uses of the permission (tagging snaps and the existing arrival alerts); a `prebuild` is required for a change to it to reach the native projects.

## Known limitations

- The counter shows the whole library's snap count, not a per-session count, so it never resets between capture sessions.
- Capturing never files a snap anywhere. Turning snaps into a movie is a separate, deliberate act that starts in the [Snap library](snaps.md) or on a [template](movie-templates.md) and finishes on [the movie screen](movie.md).
